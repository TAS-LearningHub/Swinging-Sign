Project choice

Three pathways, one evidence rhythm

Your teacher controls which project is required, alternate or additional. Learn the same design thinking while keeping every local practical fact explicit.

Primary pathway

Swinging Sign

Develop a sign that communicates clearly and connects a hanging plaque to an approved support system.

Teacher to confirm: The supplied photograph guides appearance analysis. The exact plan, stock, joints, mounting and practical sequence are Teacher to confirm.

Alternate or additional

LED tea-light holder

Explore how geometry, support and surface finish can frame a small LED light as a decorative product.

Teacher to confirm: LED light only. Any flame policy, plan, stock, joining and mounting method are Teacher to confirm.

Alternate or additional

Scrolled pot-plant holder

Explore repeated curves, visual balance and support relationships in a freestanding holder concept.

Teacher to confirm: Approved pot, loading, stability, stock, joints, scrolls and construction method are Teacher to confirm.

What stays common: purpose and user, criteria and constraints, communication, material reasoning, planning, teacher approval, quality evidence, testing and evaluation.

Photo-use boundary: the two holder photographs were supplied by the teacher and authorised for this course release on 31 August 2026. Do not reuse them outside this course without separate permission.
