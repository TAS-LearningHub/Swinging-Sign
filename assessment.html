Assessment orientation

Know the evidence. Wait for the official task notice.

The Drive guide contains a proposed assessment split, but no approved notification, due date, criteria sheet or submission method was supplied. This page is orientation only.

Teacher to confirm: whether this unit is formally assessed; task number; due date; final weighting; exact marking criteria; submission method; late-work conditions; adjustments and feedback arrangements.

Evidence this course helps you build

Design communication

Brief, research, criteria, concepts, vector/CAD evidence, annotations, peer feedback and justified decisions.

Planning and management

Material reasoning, layout, broad production sequence, approval checkpoints and resource decisions.

Practical and quality evidence

Only teacher-authorised production records, observable quality checks, approved test evidence and changes.

Evaluation

A balanced judgement against confirmed criteria, interpreted feedback and a justified improvement.

What this page does not do

  • It is not a formal assessment notification.
  • It does not publish an answer key or marking schedule.
  • It does not prove submission, teacher observation or achievement.
  • It does not turn device-local activity or quiz records into formal marks.
