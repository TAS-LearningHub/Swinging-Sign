My folio

Keep the decisions and evidence that matter.

Your work saves on this device. Export a backup regularly and follow your teacher's direction for formal submission.

1. Design brief and context

Explain the need, intended user or audience, context and the problem your approved pathway should address.

2. Product research

Compare at least two relevant products. Separate visible observations from assumptions and questions needing confirmation.

3. Criteria and constraints

Write clear, testable success criteria and the confirmed limits the project must work within.

4. Concept options

Describe or import evidence of multiple ideas. Explain how feedback changed one decision.

JPG, PNG or WebP under 6 MB. A compressed copy stays browser-local and is included in exports.

5. Final proposal and annotations

Explain the selected idea, visual hierarchy, material intentions and the information still awaiting approval.

6. Material reasoning

Justify a provisional choice from teacher-approved options using relevant properties and one trade-off.

7. Production plan and approval checkpoint

Record the broad sequence, evidence needed at each checkpoint and the result of teacher review.

JPG, PNG or WebP under 6 MB. A compressed copy stays browser-local and is included in exports.

8. Process record

Record what happened during approved practical work, including changes, resource decisions and one useful image.

JPG, PNG or WebP under 6 MB. A compressed copy stays browser-local and is included in exports.

9. Quality evidence

Compare the result with approved references or criteria. Explain one correction or monitoring decision.

JPG, PNG or WebP under 6 MB. A compressed copy stays browser-local and is included in exports.

10. Finish and function check

Record observable surface, stability, clearance and use evidence from the teacher-approved check.

JPG, PNG or WebP under 6 MB. A compressed copy stays browser-local and is included in exports.

11. Evaluation against criteria

Make a balanced judgement using specific evidence, not preference alone.

JPG, PNG or WebP under 6 MB. A compressed copy stays browser-local and is included in exports.

12. Feedback and future improvement

Identify the strongest learning, interpret feedback and justify one high-priority improvement.

Folio controls

Submission boundary: saving or exporting is not submission. Use the exact submission method supplied by your teacher.
